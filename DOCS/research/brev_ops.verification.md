## Corrected claims

- **brev_ops-1**: Brev publishes no fixed price list; rates vary by GPU, provider and region and are shown live by `brev search --sort price`. On third-party estimates, 60 dollars buys about 17 h of H100 (3.50/h), 60-80 h of A10G (0.75-1.00/h) or about 136 h of L4 (0.44/h). No L40S price was verified; read it from `brev search` before budgeting.
- **brev_ops-8**: Nemotron 3.5 Lightning (30B total, 3B active, released 2026-08-11) is the efficient agent model of the 3.5 family; Super VL 120B-A12B and Bolt 74B-A9B followed in October 2026. Under NIM one GPU needs 66 GB for BF16 or 32 GB for W4A16: a single L40S runs W4A16 only, an A100 80GB or H100 runs either, a 24 GB card runs neither. NVFP4 NIM profiles are Blackwell-only.
- **brev_ops-11**: Hosted Nemotron is rate-limited. build.nvidia.com (base URL integrate.api.nvidia.com/v1) publishes no figure; NVIDIA says limits vary per model, and forum users report 1,000 credits and 40 requests/min. OpenRouter free variants (`nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning:free`, `nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b:free`) allow 20 requests/min and 50 requests/day, or 1,000/day once 10 credits have been purchased.

## Missed by the researcher

1. Newer Nemotron 3.5 agent models exist (October 2026): Super VL 120B-A12B and Bolt 74B-A9B-24GB, the latter "designed to run everyday agentic tasks locally, using a native 2-bit quantization to fit in 24GB of memory" (its card still recommends A100, H100 or Blackwell for vLLM). https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-74B-A9B-24GB
2. Outside NIM, the Lightning NVFP4 checkpoint runs on H100 and A100 through W4A16 kernels in vLLM, and there is a llama.cpp GGUF (`ggml-org/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B-A3B-GGUF:Q4_K_M`) and an Ollama tag whose context scales with VRAM (under 24 GB: 4K; 24-48 GB: 32K; 48 GB+: 256K). The "not a 24 GB card" limit is NIM-specific. https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B-A3B-NVFP4
3. Brev has its own NIM deployment guide: it recommends an L40S 48GB or A100 80GB, uses `NGC_CLI_API_KEY`, port 8000, and `brev port-forward my-instance --port 8000:8000`. Brev's GPU reference lists A10G and L4 at 22GB and L40S at 44GB, which tightens any "fits in 24 GB" estimate. https://docs.nvidia.com/brev/guides/inference-deployment/deploying-nims ; https://docs.nvidia.com/brev/reference/gpu-types
4. The skill file also shows a middle option between private and public: `brev ports open my-instance 8888 --protocol http --authorize me@example.com` (per-email access) and `--allow <CIDR>` for TCP/UDP. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/brevdev/brev-cli/main/.agents/skills/brev-cli/SKILL.md

## Overall

The Brev operational facts (billing, stop/restart behaviour, tunnels, organizations, API keys) are reliable: every quote was found verbatim in NVIDIA's docs. Three findings need edits before presenting: the L40S price range rests on a source that could not be located, the OpenRouter slug for free Lightning is wrong as written, and "current Nemotron for agents" omits the October 2026 releases.
