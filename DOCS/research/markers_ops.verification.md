## Corrected claims

**markers_ops-2.** Sen4CAP's harvest week fell within one week of the reference for 69-86% of parcels and within two weeks for 81-91% in five of six countries over 2018 and 2019; Spain reached 53% and 69% in both years. Only the 2019 figures come from a stratified random sample of about 250 Planet-interpreted parcels per country (Lithuania 270). The 2018 references were mixed: Czechia 338 Planet parcels, Netherlands 249, Lithuania and Romania 250, Italy only 33 (against 240 farmer-reported parcels the Italian figure was 41%), and Spain 459 farmer-reported parcels with no Planet reference. Summer crops in 2019 ranged from 65% (Italy, n=51) to 88% (Czechia, n=40) within one week, excluding Lithuania (60%, n=5); summer-crop non-detection was 0-13%. The report does not itself state that the summer group contains maize.

**markers_ops-4.** EU paying agencies that use "checks by monitoring" (15 of 66 agencies in five Member States in 2019) assign every parcel a green, yellow or red flag. The Commission set initial tolerances of 5% false red and 10-20% false green, and a fixed escalation for yellow parcels (check images, ask the farmer, then inspect), scaled by money at stake (EUR 50 and EUR 250 thresholds, 5% sample). In the four agencies the auditors visited, for the Basic Payment Scheme in 2019, 2.9-11.2% of parcels were flagged red or yellow after automated processing and 0.1-1.0% were inspected in the field (Italy's field share unknown). The auditors noted the guidelines set no minimum sample numbers for quality control.

## Missed or understated

1. **Sen4CAP 2018 validation is weaker than the headline table suggests.** Italy 2018 "76%" is 25 of 33 Planet parcels; against 240 farmer-reported parcels it was 41% within one week (26% more than three weeks off). Spain 2018 is farmer-reported dates only. https://www.esa-sen4cap.org/wp-content/uploads/files/14_Sen4CAP_VR_v1.2.pdf
2. **75.4% is not the corn accuracy, and the CDL has no silage class.** In the 2025 national accuracy workbook, 75.4% is the "FSA Crops" row (all crop-specific categories). Corn (class 1) has producer's accuracy 92.2% and user's accuracy 91.2%. No category name contains "silage". https://www.nass.usda.gov/sites/default/files/Research_and_Science/Cropland/docs/CDL_2025_accuracy_assessments.zip
3. **Checks by monitoring was a voluntary pilot; it has been succeeded by the Area Monitoring System.** The Commission lists the AMS as an element of the IACS under the CAP 2023-27, so the 2019 figures describe the predecessor scheme. https://agriculture.ec.europa.eu/common-agricultural-policy/financing-cap/assurance-and-audit/managing-payments_en
4. **The Sen4CAP documents cited are from 2021 and validate the 2018-2019 seasons.** The project site lists Sen4CAP system version 5.0 with a release notice dated 6 October 2026, so defaults and markers may have changed since ATBD v1.5. https://www.esa-sen4cap.org/

## Overall

All eight sources exist and every quoted number was found in them; no fabricated citations. Two findings need rewording for scope rather than for numbers: the Sen4CAP 2018 sample design (markers_ops-2) and the reach of the EU flag scheme (markers_ops-4). The NASS and Statistics Canada figures can be quoted as given, with the caveat that the CDL 75.4% is an all-crop figure, not corn.
