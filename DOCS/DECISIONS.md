- 2026-11-15T00:53Z [cc-varun] Scoring year is 2025; nothing is fitted, tuned or selected on 2025 values. Why: the organizers posted the 2025 state grain and silage acres as the table results are evaluated against (DOCS/research/data_sources.md, section 0.4).
- 2026-11-15T00:53Z [cc-varun] Add library odc-stac (with pystac-client and planetary-computer). Why: the organizer starter notebook and the team's Keeboh notebook already load Sentinel-2 this way, it needs no key, and it is installed on Keeboh.
- 2026-11-15T00:53Z [cc-varun] Field-level training labels come from Quebec's declared-parcel database; the organizers' 44 Maryland field-years are test only. Why: 12 silage field-years on one farm cannot train a model without overfitting.
- 2026-11-15T00:53Z [cc-varun] Per-field Sentinel-2 series are pulled at 20 m with a one-pixel inner buffer. Why: the moisture and residue bands are 20 m native, and it keeps each download small enough for the 10 GB Keeboh workspace.
- 2026-11-15T01:36Z [cc-varun] State silage acres are scored by an expanding-window backtest that stops at 2024; the acreage history comes from the organizer file and the context (milk cows, cattle, yield forecasts, prices) from the team Quick Stats folder in data/. Why: one panel, one rule for what was public by mid-November, and 2025 stays held out.
- 2026-11-15T02:16Z [cc-daniel] County weather comes from NOAA nClimGrid-Daily county averages, reduced to county-month and county-season tables, not gridMET. Why: NOAA publishes ready-made daily county tables that need no sign-in or raster work, so the pull runs with the standard library on a laptop and on Keeboh; the daily files (about 1 GB for 2000 to 2026) are not stored.
- 2026-11-15T02:16Z [cc-daniel] Context pulls start in 2000, RMA files keep corn rows only, and the per-state county drought files and yearly RMA files are stored gzipped. Why: 2000 is the first Drought Monitor year and covers the silage shock years 2002, 2006, 2011, 2012, 2019 and 2022; gzip keeps those two sets near 90 MB in a checkout.
- 2026-11-15T02:44Z [cc-varun] The state signal uses a fixed sample of 10 km areas per state, drawn with probability proportional to corn acres and reused every year, and reads the year-to-year change of the satellite silage share, not its level. Why: the field model has a false-alarm rate of about 12% on the Maryland test, which would swamp the level in grain states; a constant bias cancels in the change.
- 2026-11-15T02:40Z [cc-manraj] State acreage history is the kit file plus the team Quick Stats folder (kit wins on overlap), giving every year 2013 to 2025 with no API key; the "OTHER STATES" row is dropped. Why: the kit has no state rows for 2017 and 2022, and "OTHER STATES" has in-season forecasts but a final of 0, which inflated the grain baseline error.
- 2026-11-15T02:40Z [cc-manraj] The silage feature set is chosen by a rule fixed before scoring: the smallest set whose mean state error on 2017 to 2024 is within 0.3 points of the best. It chose reversion_only. Why: the stress features (drought, crop condition, insured losses, yield shock) have the expected signs and cut the US-total error (3.1% against 3.9%) but not the state error (13.8% against 13.1%), and a rule set in advance keeps 2025 out of the choice.
- 2026-11-15T02:40Z [cc-manraj] Intervals are quantiles of the backtest's own log errors within three state size classes; the US interval uses the spread of the backtest's US-total errors (8 years, normal assumption). Why: no distribution to assume at state level, and leave-one-year-out coverage is 0.79 and 0.88 for the 80% and 90% intervals.
- 2026-11-15T02:40Z [cc-manraj] RMA insured-silage acres and "planted minus NASS grain forecast" are not used as silage predictors. Why: both are worse than same as last year on 2014 to 2024 (19.4% and 17.2% against 14.3%; test_models/manrajModel/signal_scan.py).
- 2026-11-15T02:40Z [cc-manraj] RMA cause-of-loss files are used at today's vintage. Why: past November vintages are not published; loss_share for past years is therefore slightly more complete than it was in season, and it is not in the selected set.
- 2026-11-15T03:09Z [cc-manraj] The open season's state planted and grain acres are parsed from NASS's Crop Production text reports (crop0926.txt, crop1126.txt), stored in data/nass/reports/. Why: neither the kit nor the Quick Stats folder has 2026 rows, and without them the 2026 grain forecast was last year's figure (90.9M against NASS's 88.5M).
- 2026-11-15T03:09Z [cc-manraj] Hay stocks, hay production, milk price and the planted-minus-grain change (plain, silage-weighted, and as a cap) stay out of the silage model. Why: on 2017 to 2024 each is within 0.1 point of reversion_only (13.1%) or worse (test_models/manrajModel/silage_scan.py); the selection rule keeps the smaller set.
- 2026-11-15T03:09Z [cc-manraj] Field rates carry 90% bootstrap intervals and earliness is measured by cutting each Maryland series at an as-of date, with the Quebec fit unchanged. Why: 44 test field-years on one farm; a point rate alone overstates what is known.
