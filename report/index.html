Limits

What the numbers do not show.

  • Truth is state totals only; field rates come from proxy labels.
  • Field labels cover one Maryland farm and one Quebec year.
  • Harvest calendars and soil colour differ by region; evidence is relative to each field's own season.
  • Satellite backtests with a fixed Quebec classifier are retrospective; the classifier may use later training labels.
  • Satellite change, sampled states: — against — for same as last year.
  • RMA loss files are today's vintage, not the vintage of each past November.
  • 2025 is excluded from fitting, selection and this backtest.
  • Test field-years cover one farm: field-cluster intervals do not capture regional transfer uncertainty.
  • Unassessable fields count as grain for scoring only; no observed harvest is implied.
  • Early drops are provisional; feature availability does not confirm harvest.
  • Response time excludes the imagery download.
