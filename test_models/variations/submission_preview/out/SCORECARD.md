## Where each number comes from

- Grain `NASS` (Tier C): NASS's own November harvested-for-grain forecast, passed through (current deliverable).
- Grain `A-G3` (Tier A): g3_fsa_ratio_multi: FSA grain-intended acres x median NASS/FSA ratio of 3 years.
- Grain `g9` (Tier A): g9_national_reconciliation: A-G3's FSA states scaled to a national FSA x NASS/FSA ratio.
- Silage `SC` (Tier C): smoothed_chopping (current deliverable; NASS yield and grain-acre forecasts).
- Silage `SC_A` (Tier A): SC with a Drought Monitor D2+ exposure (Tier A twin).
- Silage `SC_B` (Tier B): SC with last year's final grain acres in the exposure (Tier B twin).
- Silage `A-S3` (Tier A): s3_fsa_residual (A-S3), corrected FSA dates.
- Silage `v1_geo` (Tier C): sqrt(A-S3 x SC).
- NASS pass-through and SC: the reproduced deliverable for 2017-2025 and 2026.
- A-G3 2017-2024: `ownModels/monthly/out/monthly_by_state_year.csv` (corrected FSA dates). g9 2017-2024: `variations/grain_variants/out/by_state_year.csv`. SC_A, SC_B, A-S3, v1_geo 2017-2024: `variations/silage_combos/out/backtest_by_state_year.csv`.
- 2025 for g9, SC_A, SC_B, A-S3 and v1_geo is in no saved file (those folders kept 2025 out); for A-G3 only the grain folder's pre-fix `score_2025_information_only.csv` has it. All six are recomputed here by importing the owners' code unchanged, after checking that the same recomputation reproduces their saved 2017-2024 and 2026 rows:
  - A-G3: max |diff| vs saved 2017-2024 = 0.050 acre, vs saved 2026 = 0.002 acre; 2025 FSA file AwCPYHfbDXJx.zip (data 2025-09-02).
  - g9: max |diff| vs saved 2017-2024 = 0.050 acre, vs saved 2026 = 0.005 acre; 2025 FSA file AwCPYHfbDXJx.zip (data 2025-09-02).
  - SC: max |diff| vs saved 2017-2024 = 0.050 acre, vs saved 2026 = 0.049 acre, vs the deliverable 2017-2025 = 0.000000 acre.
  - SC_A: max |diff| vs saved 2017-2024 = 0.050 acre, vs saved 2026 = 0.049 acre.
  - SC_B: max |diff| vs saved 2017-2024 = 0.050 acre, vs saved 2026 = 0.049 acre.
  - A-S3: max |diff| vs saved 2017-2024 = 0.050 acre, vs saved 2026 = 0.050 acre.
  - v1_geo: max |diff| vs saved 2017-2024 = 0.050 acre, vs saved 2026 = 0.045 acre.
- 2026 A-G3 and g9 points and 80% intervals: `variations/grain_variants/out/forecast_2026.csv` (run through the monthly harness with corrected FSA dates; equal to the recomputation). The grain folder's older files from before the FSA date fix, `forecast_2026.csv` and `score_2025_information_only.csv`, keep 6 significant digits; rounded to that precision the recomputation equals them (max |diff| 0.0 and 0.0 acres; unrounded 25 and 49 acres). So the date fix changes neither the 2025 nor the 2026 A-G3 numbers at 15 Nov (both use the same FSA file before and after).
- The 2017-2024 means are computed exactly as `kernelag.deliverable.summarize` computes them: the mean of per-year values already rounded to 0.01. This can differ by 0.01 from a folder's own unrounded mean (v1_geo silage |US| is 3.69 here, 3.68 in silage_combos: 3.6850 vs 3.6835).
- 2026 silage intervals: SC from the deliverable (state 80/90%, US 80/90%); SC_A, SC_B, A-S3, v1_geo from silage_combos (state 80/90%, US 80% only). Grain 90% intervals exist only for the NASS pass-through.

## Caveats

- 8 backtest years. Differences of a few hundredths of a point between combinations are not distinguishable from noise; the silage options span about 0.3 point of state WAPE and 0.7 point of |US|.
- The ranking rule weights the four numbers equally; another weighting can reorder close rows.
- A-G3, g9, SC_A, SC_B, A-S3 and v1_geo were selected or tuned on 2012-2024 or 2017-2024 by their own folders; 2025 entered none of them. The 2025 columns are a single year.
- Monthly grids: SC, SC_A, SC_B and v1_geo have saved predictions for 15 Sep / Oct / Nov only (silage_combos); NASS, A-G3, g9 and A-S3 have all six dates (monthly and grain_variants folders).
- Current-vintage inputs throughout (a replay, not archived real-time runs), as in the deliverable.
